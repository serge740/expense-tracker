Phase 5 — Reminder experience

How reminders look and behave in the app. Nothing here is saved. Try the buttons inside the phone.

The popup appears by itself when the app opens or comes to the front, when you tap a reminder push, and once a minute while you use the app. It shows the most urgent first. Closing it with the × does not count as an answer, so the reminder comes back on schedule. Mark paid, Snooze and Dismiss are answers.
Mark paid does not just tick a box: it records the payment on the debt (amount and wallet), so your balances and history stay right. The reminder completes by itself.
Push buttons (Snooze 10 min, Snooze 1 h, Open) open the app and apply your choice. They are plain notification actions, so no rebuild is needed; I will test them on a real phone.
Quiet hours move to your account so they work while the app is closed. Your current quiet hours on this phone are copied over once.
