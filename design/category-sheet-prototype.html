How it works

      
    
        
  • Step 1: 10 categories, all on one screen, your 3 most used on top.
    • 
        
  • Step 2: the items you really pay for. Each category has group tabs (Food: Shopping, Drinks, Eating out), so nothing is a long wall.
    • 
        
  • Type in the box to search every group at once. Brand names work: "bolt" finds Ride app.
    • 
        
  • What you type or pick is kept under "Yours", shown first next time, and nobody else sees it.
    • 
        
  • Only the item is saved on the transaction; the group is just for finding it.
    •
