Phase 7 — Home dashboard

The new Home. Nothing here is saved. Try the Include loans switch, the filter chips, tap a card, and open Net worth.

What is new on Home. A Include loans switch under the month figures, cards for what you owe and what you are owed, a Needs attention list (overdue pay dates and scheduled items waiting for you), a Net worth line, and a recent feed you can filter. The balance header, quick actions and budget stay as they are.
Include loans (on by default, remembered). Borrowed money is never income. With it on, loan repayments you make count as expense, lending counts as expense and repayments you receive count as income. With it off, every loan movement is left out so the month shows only everyday money. A separate "Loans" line always shows how much of the month was loans.
The feed filters work on the server: Income and Expense leave out borrowed money and respect the loans switch; Debt = borrowed and repayments you made; Credit = lent and repayments you received; Scheduled = upcoming dates first, then what a schedule has posted. Your choice is remembered, and "See all" opens History with the same filter.
Net worth never mixes currencies: it is shown for each currency (wallets + owed to you − you owe). A converted total can come later from the exchange rates already in the app.
