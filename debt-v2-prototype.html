Phase 3 — Debt & Credit v2

Interactive mock of the screens that change. Nothing here is saved. Tap around: record a payment, add / move a pay date, forgive, archive.

How the pay-date status works. Payments are applied to pay dates oldest first. Each date is Paid when the money paid so far covers it, Partial when it covers part, Overdue when the time has passed and it is not covered. Moving a pay date never edits the old one: it stays in the history as Moved, and a new one is added.
Debt / Credit wallets are not real wallets. They are protected summary cards worked out from your debts. You cannot pay from them, delete them or transfer to them.
Repeating does not apply here. Each pay date is added by hand.
